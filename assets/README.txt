Put your images in this folder with these exact names:

bg.jpg      - page background texture/photo
photo.webp  - big photo on the left of the lock screen
flower.webp - small flower image (top-right of the photo)
paper.webp  - torn paper texture behind the password box
side.webp   - dried rose / photo on the left of the letter page
art.webp    - faint artwork at the top-right of the letter
clock.webp  - clock face (1199 x 1312) for the live clock

Missing images are simply skipped; everything still works.
(Extract them from your old file: each data:image/...;base64 string can be
decoded into a file, or just use your original image files.)
